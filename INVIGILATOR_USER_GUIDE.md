# 📘 MSE Exam Attendance Portal — Invigilator User Guide

Welcome to the **MSE Exam Attendance Portal**. This guide is designed to help you quickly mark and manage student attendance during examinations with maximum speed, zero paperwork, and minimal effort.

---

## 🌟 Key Advantages of Using This Portal

1. **⚡ Fast & Paperless Workflow**
   - Say goodbye to manual paper sheets, signature collection, physical tallying, and misplaced registers.
2. **🪑 Row & Bench Walking Alignment (Seating Plan Order)**
   - Sort students in the exact physical bench order (column-by-column) matching your room's seating layout. You can simply walk down rows and mark attendance in sequence without constantly hunting for roll numbers.
3. **📊 Real-time Progress & Auto-Counting**
   - Live tally of **Present**, **Absent**, and **Unmarked** students updates in real-time, eliminating manual headcounts.
4. **🔍 Instant Search & Multiple Sort Views**
   - Instantly find any student by enrollment number, student name, or subject code.
5. **🔒 Data Integrity & Room Locking**
   - Once marked, records are securely stored on the cloud. Admin lock protections ensure records cannot be tampered with after exam hours.
6. **📱 Mobile & Tablet Friendly**
   - Fully optimized for smartphones, tablets, and laptops with one-tap action buttons.

---

## 🚀 Step-by-Step Instructions for Invigilators

### Step 1: Log In to the Portal
1. Open the portal URL: [MSE Attendance Portal](https://frontend-nine-green-42.vercel.app/)
2. Enter your assigned **Username** and **Password**.
3. Tap **Sign In to Portal**.
   > *Tip: You can change your password anytime by clicking the 🔑 (Key) icon in the top header.*

---

### Step 2: Select Exam Date, Session & Room
At the top of the **Mark Attendance** tab:
1. **Exam Date**: Tap **Select Date** (only current and upcoming exam dates are shown for invigilators).
2. **Session**: Select the appropriate exam session (e.g., `Morning` or `Evening`).
3. **Room No.**: Choose your assigned examination room.
   - The dropdown displays the total students and how many have been marked so far (e.g., `Room 101 (0/32 marked)`).

---

### Step 3: Choose Your Preferred Walking Order (Sort Toggle)
Next to the search bar, tap the **Sort** dropdown to match your invigilation style:

| Sort Mode | Best Used For |
| :--- | :--- |
| **🪑 Seating Plan Order (Rows/Benches)** *(Recommended)* | Walking down benches/rows column-by-column. Students show a **Seat #N** badge matching the seating chart. |
| **🔢 Roll Number (Default)** | Calling out roll numbers or verifying student admit cards numerically. |
| **🔤 Student Name (A-Z)** | Quickly looking up students alphabetically by name. |
| **📚 Subject Code** | Grouping students if multiple subjects or sections share the same hall. |
| **⏳ Unmarked First** | Reviewing remaining unmarked candidates at the end of the session. |

---

### Step 4: Mark Attendance
For each student, choose one of the following methods:

- **Individual Marking**: Tap **✓ Present** (green) or **✕ Absent** (red) next to the student's name. Tapping an active button toggles it back to unmarked if needed.
- **Bulk Strategy (Quickest Method)**:
  1. Tap **Mark All Present** on the bottom floating bar.
  2. Walk down rows and tap **✕ Absent** only for empty seats.
  3. This takes under **60 seconds** for an entire hall!

---

### Step 5: Save & Verify
1. Observe the **Live Bottom Bar**:
   - Check the progress bar reaches **100% marked** (Unmarked count should ideally be 0).
2. Tap **Save Attendance**.
3. A green confirmation banner will confirm: `"Successfully saved X attendance records!"`.

---

## 💡 Pro Tips & Best Practices

- **Instant Student Lookup**: If a student is sitting in an unexpected seat, type the last 3-4 digits of their Roll Number in the search bar.
- **Verify Before Finishing**: Switch the sort dropdown to **⏳ Unmarked First** before leaving the room to ensure no student was accidentally skipped.
- **Locked Rooms**: If your room displays a **🔒 Locked** banner, the attendance has been finalized by the administrator. Contact your exam coordinator if edits are required.
- **Battery & Data Friendly**: The app operates with minimal data consumption and works smoothly even on mobile hotspot connections.
